What posting a run does
A draft run is a calculation nobody has committed to — recalculating throws the payslips away and works them out again from whatever the register now says. Posting writes one balanced entry for the whole run and freezes the payslips. Net pay is credited to a payable, not to the bank: posting recognises a wage bill, and the bank is touched when the money actually leaves. Voiding writes a reversing entry and never unpicks the original.
