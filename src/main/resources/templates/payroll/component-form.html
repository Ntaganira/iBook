How it is treated

Both of these decide real money. Neither is inferred from the name.
Off means PAYE is worked out as though this allowance were not there. Some allowances are exempt only up to a limit — this switch is all or nothing, so an allowance that is partly exempt has to be split into two.
Pension, maternity, occupational hazards and medical are all worked out on the same base. An allowance one scheme counts and another does not cannot be told apart here — turn it off and add it back another way if that matters.
Retiring it stops it being offered on new records and stops it being worked out on a run. It stays on the people already carrying it, and on every payslip it has already appeared on.
