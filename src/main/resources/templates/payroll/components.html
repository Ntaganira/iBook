Taxable and pensionable are decisions, not guesses
Whether an allowance is taxed, and whether it counts towards contributions, are questions of tax and social security law that vary by allowance and change over time. Nothing here infers them from the name — both start off included, which is the treatment that cannot under-declare, and both are yours to correct.
A deduction needs somewhere for the money to go
Money taken off somebody's pay has not vanished — it is either still owed to a third party or it has paid down something they owed the company. A staff loan repayment credits the receivable it reduces, not an income account. Without an account the payroll entry would not balance.
