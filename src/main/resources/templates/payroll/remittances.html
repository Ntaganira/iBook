Payslips Record a payment
Posting payroll only recognises the debt
PAYE, RSSB and CBHI sit on the balance sheet as liabilities until somebody actually pays them — which is right, because it is other people's money being held. Recording a payment here debits the liability and credits the bank, and that is the entry that clears it. Without it the balance sheet goes on carrying deductions that left the building months ago.
