Rates and return
A part-payment of the bill, to the state instead of the supplier
Issuing a certificate records the withheld amount against the bill as a payment whose destination is the withholding liability rather than a bank account. That is exactly what it is, and it keeps the bill's balance, the aging report and the payables total all agreeing with the ledger instead of being out by the amount withheld. Only bills can be withheld from: a direct expense is already paid in full, so there is nothing left to hold back.
