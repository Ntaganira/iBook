Capture always produces a draft
A figure read off a document is a claim about a piece of paper, not a fact about the books, so it never posts — it becomes a draft expense for somebody to look at. The arithmetic is checked but never corrected: if the subtotal and tax do not reach the printed total, that is flagged and conversion refused, because which of the three figures is wrong is not something this page can tell. One document converts once, and the file is attached to the expense it becomes.
