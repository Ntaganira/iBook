Saying what a line is posts it
Choosing an account writes one balanced entry — the bank account against the account chosen, in the direction the statement says the money went — and stamps the entry number on the line so the same movement cannot be entered twice. Setting a line aside records that somebody looked and decided it needed nothing, which is usually because the movement is already in the books from the invoice or bill side; that is different from, and far more useful than, a line nobody ever dealt with.
