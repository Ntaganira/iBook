Uncategorised Reconciliation
This is a file import, not a bank connection
Nothing here dials out to anybody. A live feed needs credentials and an agreement with the bank, and neither exists in this application — so no balance updates by itself and no transaction arrives without somebody bringing it. What does exist is the CSV every bank will export, and reading that is what makes categorising and reconciling possible.
Importing posts nothing
Lines arrive uncategorised and stay that way until somebody says what each one is, because a payment out could be rent, a loan repayment, a drawing or a theft and only a person knows which. Nothing is guessed: there is no rule engine and nothing learns from past decisions, because a wrong guess that posts silently is worse than a line that waits. A row that cannot be read is counted and reported rather than dropped, since a statement that quietly lost three rows would reconcile to the wrong figure with nothing to say why.

Bring a statement in

A CSV exported from your bank.
Cash and bank accounts only.
CSV or tab-separated. The header row is read to find the columns, so the order does not matter — no two banks export the same shape.
What it looks for: a column whose name contains "date"; one containing description, narrative, details, particulars or memo; either a single amount column, or a pair naming credit and debit — or money in and money out, or deposit and withdrawal. A reference and a running balance are used if present. Thousands separators, currency symbols and brackets for negatives are all handled, and both 1,234.56 and 1.234,56 are understood.
Bringing the same file in twice is warned about rather than refused — sometimes a statement genuinely is re-sent — but it would double every figure on it, so read the warning.
