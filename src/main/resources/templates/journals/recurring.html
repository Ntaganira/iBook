A template, not an entry
A schedule posts nothing itself. Each occurrence is a real journal entry dated the day it was owed, raised as a draft unless the schedule opts into auto-posting. A schedule cannot start unless its debits equal its credits, and the balance is checked again as every entry is written — a schedule that stopped balancing is refused rather than quietly writing a one-sided entry each cycle.

With auto-posting on, the nightly sweep writes to the ledger with nobody in the loop, and the only brake is a cap of 24 catch-up entries per schedule per night. Leave it off unless the entry is genuinely the same every cycle.
Stopping a schedule leaves every entry it raised exactly where it is, posted ones included — an entry in the ledger is a fact. A schedule that ran wrong has to be corrected entry by entry.
