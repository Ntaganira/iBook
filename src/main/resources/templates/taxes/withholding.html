Withholding is not a cost
The whole of the bill is still the company's expense; part of it is simply paid to the RRA rather than to the supplier. So issuing a certificate posts a debit to accounts payable and a credit to the withholding liability, and touches no expense account — it moves a debt from one creditor to another. It is recorded as a part-payment of the bill, which is what keeps the bill's balance, the aging report and the payables total all saying the same thing.
