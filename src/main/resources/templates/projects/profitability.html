Job Costing Project budgets
Two measures, never added together
The ledger columns are what the books say about this job — the tagged revenue and expense lines, nothing else. The time columns are what the job is thought to have consumed. They are not added up, because the wages behind booked hours already sit in the ledger through payroll: adding the cost of time on top would count the same wages twice.
