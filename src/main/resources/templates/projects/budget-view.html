A project budget never reaches the ledger. Approving it only freezes the figures, so a comparison is not measuring against something still being edited. Actual comes from the ledger lines tagged to the job on Job Costing — a job whose costs nobody has tagged compares against zero and will look under budget when it is not.
Variance is reported as favourable or adverse rather than raw arithmetic, because earning more than planned and spending less than planned are both good and carry opposite signs.
