The claim

Only a fixed-price job with a customer and an agreed price above zero can be claimed against.
Cumulative, not this claim's share. Forty per cent after a twenty-five per cent claim asks for the fifteen in between.
Used by the flat-amount method only: a milestone payment, a deposit, or a release of retention.

This wording goes onto the invoice line, so write it for the customer.
Only a fixed-price job is billed this way — a job charged by the hour bills its approved time instead, and the two can never both apply. A claim is cumulative: "40% complete" means forty per cent of the contract has been earned in total, so this claim asks for forty per cent less everything already claimed. Raising it writes a draft invoice, and nothing reaches the ledger until that invoice is posted.
