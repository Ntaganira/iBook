Tagging is not a posting
A tag says which job a line belongs to. The account, the date and the amount stay exactly as the document wrote them, so no trial balance, profit and loss or VAT return moves because a line was tagged. Only revenue and expense lines carry a job: the bank side of a payment is money moving between two of your own pockets, not the cost of anything.
